# It's All Greek – Brand Guide

Version: 1.0

This document defines the personality, values and communication style of the It's All Greek brand.

When making design, UX or copywriting decisions, always follow this document.

---

# Brand Identity

It's All Greek is a modern Greek restaurant that brings the atmosphere, hospitality and flavors of Greece to the Netherlands.

The experience should feel authentic, warm and premium without becoming formal or intimidating.

We serve traditional Greek dishes with a modern presentation in a contemporary restaurant.

---

# Mission

To create an authentic Greek dining experience where guests feel welcome from the moment they arrive until they leave.

Our goal is not only to serve food, but to create memorable experiences.

---

# Vision

Become the most recognizable modern Greek restaurant in the region through outstanding food, hospitality and atmosphere.

---

# Core Values

- Authentic
- Welcoming
- Passionate
- Honest
- Quality first
- Family-oriented
- Consistent
- Professional

---

# Personality

If It's All Greek were a person, it would be:

- Friendly
- Confident
- Stylish
- Relaxed
- Passionate about food
- Proud of its Greek heritage
- Helpful
- Honest

Never arrogant.

Never overly formal.

Never loud.

---

# Target Audience

Primary audience:

- Couples
- Families
- Friends
- Local residents
- Professionals
- People looking for quality dining

Age:

25–65

---

# Customer Experience

Every visitor should feel:

- Welcome
- Comfortable
- Appreciated
- Relaxed
- Hungry
- Curious
- Inspired to return

---

# Tone of Voice

Always write in a tone that is:

- Warm
- Friendly
- Clear
- Personal
- Professional
- Inviting

Avoid:

- Corporate language
- Marketing buzzwords
- Long paragraphs
- Aggressive sales language
- Clickbait

---

# Writing Style

Use:

- Short sentences
- Natural language
- Positive wording
- Clear calls-to-action

Do not:

- Overcomplicate explanations
- Use filler words
- Exaggerate
- Sound robotic

---

# Visual Personality

The visual identity should communicate:

- Freshness
- Mediterranean lifestyle
- Authentic cuisine
- Premium quality
- Warm hospitality

The website should immediately create the feeling of entering the restaurant.

---

# Photography Style

Photography should feel:

- Natural
- Warm
- Inviting
- Authentic
- High-end

Avoid:

- Artificial lighting
- Generic stock photos
- Empty restaurant photos
- Overedited images

Show:

- Fresh food
- Happy guests
- Interior atmosphere
- Details
- Chefs when appropriate

---

# User Experience

Visitors should always know:

- Where they are
- What they can do next
- How to reserve
- How to order
- How to contact us

Navigation should feel effortless.

---

# Calls To Action

Preferred CTA style:

- Reserve a table
- View menu
- Order takeaway
- Contact us

CTAs should encourage action without pressure.

---

# AI Development Rules

When generating content:

Always write as if speaking directly to the customer.

Every sentence should support the brand identity.

If uncertain about wording:

Choose the option that feels more personal, warm and authentic.

Never invent company history, awards or facts.

Never make promises that cannot be verified.

When in doubt, ask instead of assume.

---

# Final Rule

Everything should make visitors feel like they are already experiencing a small piece of Greece before they even enter the restaurant.




# Language Rules

The primary website language is Dutch.

Dutch should always be used unless another language is explicitly requested.

Greek words may be used sparingly to strengthen the atmosphere, but never at the expense of readability.

English is only used for technical documentation and development.
