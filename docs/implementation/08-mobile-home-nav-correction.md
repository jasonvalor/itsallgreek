- Header logo width: about 74-80px on mobile; transparent image, no tile.
- Header top spacing: logo sits about 8px from the top of the header with a 44px hamburger hit area.
- Heading width: about 210-225px at 390px so the three lines stay compact.
- Heading size: about 34-36px at 390px.
- Heading line-height: about 0.92.
- CTA size: about 144-152px wide and 42-44px high.
- Right image: about 74-86px wide and 150-170px high, near the upper-right of the hero.
- Main image: begins around 390-405px from the top of a 390px viewport and is less tall than the previous oversized card.
- Mobile-menu top spacing: logo near 18px from top; first row in the upper region, about 115-145px from top after comparing the approved phone panel to current code.
- Menu link spacing: compact rows with about 6-10px visual row gap and 44px minimum row height.
- Icon size: 17-19px for menu rows; hamburger and close visible icons about 22-24px.
- Close-control size: 44px invisible hit area with only the blue X visible.
